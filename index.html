Invoice Match sample data

One morning of supplier invoices, checked against the order and the dock.

Nothing posts until every line matches.

Each invoice is read line by line. The supplier is found by ABN, not by name. Every line must match a line on the purchase order, be no more than was received at the dock, and cost no more than the order says. GST must be 10% of the taxable lines only; basic food is GST-free. A new bank account, a repeated invoice number or an unreadable order number stops the invoice.

Nothing is filled in from what is usual. If a value is not on the page, it says not found, and a person decides.
